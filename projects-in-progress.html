Habit Enforcer

In Progress

An uncompromising discipline and habit-building mobile tool that locks access to your phone via an unskippable overlay until daily scheduled routines are completed.

Show more
  • • Inspiration & Real-World Motivation: Created out of personal necessity to overcome lack of discipline and enforce daily consistency, specifically to guarantee daily completion of a flexibility stretching routine and habituated study sessions.
  • • Enforced Routine Scheduling: Enables users to configure strict, scheduled commitments (e.g. 15 minutes of Anki flashcards daily at 8:00 PM).
  • • System-Level Lockscreen Overlay: When a habit session triggers, the app engages an unavoidable device overlay locking the rest of the phone until the task is fulfilled, while safely whitelisting emergency contacts and essential tools.
  • • Automatic Device Release: Detects and verifies the full completion of the designated activity, immediately lifting the phone lockdown overlay.
  • • Current Progress: Core functionality (scheduling triggers, locking overlay, and activity tracking) is complete and working; current focus is on organizing premium tier feature sets and monetized workflows.
